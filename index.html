          How the hybrid works

          1. Short document? Gemini Nano explains it on your device. Nothing is uploaded — your letter stays private, it costs nothing, and it even works offline.
          2. Long document? Plainly sends it to Gemini in the cloud and says so, with the reason. Nano's context window is small; the cloud handles the big ones.
          3. Either way, the answer is labelled with the engine that actually ran it — Firebase AI Logic reports it for every request.

          Built with Firebase AI Logic hybrid inference (Gemini Nano on-device + Gemini in the cloud), Firestore, Anonymous Auth, Remote Config and App Check.
